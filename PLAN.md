# RateDeck — Plan

## Problem

Freelancers undercharge because rates feel like a guessing game. They need a **defensible number** plus language to send to clients — not another vague “charge more” thread.

## Audience

- Independent designers, engineers, and consultants
- Specialists entering a new market (city ↔ remote)
- Freelancers preparing a raise or first enterprise pitch

## Why $1 (for RateDeck itself)

- Impulse buy → converts curiosity into paid users
- Matches “tiny tool, real artifact” positioning
- Validates willingness-to-pay before a $9–$29 Pro tier

## Competition

| Alternative | Gap |
|-------------|-----|
| Glassdoor / Levels.fyi | Salaried; weak freelance + project framing |
| ChatGPT “what should I charge” | Inconsistent; no unlockable pack |
| Rate calculators (static) | No email/scripts; opaque or oversimplified |
| Coaches / communities | Slow and expensive |

RateDeck wins on **speed + transparent formula + client-ready scripts**.

## Monetization

1. **Free:** ranges, formula, positioning, comparison notes (scripts locked)
2. **$1 unlock:** Markdown, justification email, discount/raise scripts, 1 regenerate
3. **Later:** $9 niche packs (design / eng / marketing), PDF export, team seats

## GTM (first 7 days)

1. X post with screen recording of form → rate card
2. IndieHackers “I launched” + ask for rate feedback
3. Reddit r/freelance, r/forhire meta (value-first)
4. Soft PH once Stripe live + custom domain

## Metrics

- Landing → Create CTR
- Generations / day
- Unlock conversion (goal: ≥5% of generators)
- Time to first $1

## Risks

- Stripe not configured → no revenue (see MORNING_BRIEF)
- Formula feels “too low/high” for niche → iterate multipliers from feedback
- Abuse of free gens → rate-limit later
